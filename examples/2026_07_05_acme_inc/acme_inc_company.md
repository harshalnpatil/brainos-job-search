# Product

> Agent note: Build this section in order: `JD -> targeted product research -> fill in`.
> Agent note: Start by extracting the product area implied by the JD. Example prompts: What exact product surface does the role own? What users and collaborators are named? What workflows, handoffs, AI features, channels, or system constraints are implied? Then research that slice on the company site, demos, docs, help center, pricing pages, release notes, and credible videos. The goal is to explain the product area the role will actually touch, not only the company-level product catalog.

- Core product lines:
  - Include the company-wide product map briefly.
  - Then add role-relevant product detail tied to the JD's scope.
- Primary users:
  - Distinguish platform buyers from end users in the specific product area named by the JD.
- Key workflows:
  - Include the concrete workflow this role likely owns or improves.
  - If the JD mentions collaboration, AI, orchestration, digital channels, routing, handoff, automation, analytics, or platform capabilities, spell out how those workflows appear in the product.
- Differentiators:
  - Include at least 1-3 differentiators specific to the JD's product area, not only broad company claims.
  - Note important limits or open questions when evidence is thin.
