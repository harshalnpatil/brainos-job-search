#### Ask these Qs

(good base)
- If you could change anything in your product, what is one thing you would change in it?
- Imagine 6 months from now, you say I was super successful in this role. What would that look like?
- How can someone in this role fail, even if they're smart and hardworking?
- To hit these objectives, what budget will I control or influence?
- What kind of team (skills, seniority, headcount) would I have, and how quickly could we hire if gaps appear?
- Are there significant tech-debt, process, or political constraints I should know about that could block these OKRs?
- What questions do you have for me that would make you more comfortable to move me forward?
- What would my team composition be? Which other teams would I be working with?
- How has product building changed for you and your team in the last year?
- Is there anything else I should know about this role or company that I didn't ask?
- What customer or business metric would most clearly show that this PM is creating value in the role? (revenue-driving vs platform enablement function)

(for recruiter)
- From candidates you have advanced for similar roles, what consistently separated the strong ones from the rest (examples, seniority signals, domain proof)?
- Beyond the job description, what are must-haves versus nice-to-haves for this hiring manager, so I can stress the right experiences in later rounds?
- What prompted creation of this role?

(for hiring manager)
- What support would I have from you and from peers—mentorship, access to leadership, and cross-functional help?
- How do you think about the role of individual PMs on your team?
- What does success look like for someone in this role in the first 90 days?
- When you've disagreed with a PM on your team about direction or priority, how did you handle it?
- How much of your time do you spend coaching versus steering?
- What's something you've changed about how you manage based on feedback from your reports?
- What does your relationship with engineering look like, and how do you help your PMs navigate that?
